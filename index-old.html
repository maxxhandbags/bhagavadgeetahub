🙏 Gurudev's Message

Swami Harihar Ji Maharaj gave his early life to India's freedom struggle, then devoted himself to saints and the ancient scriptures. Lord Krishna's teaching in the Bhagavad Geeta became his guide, and he held that it serves all of humanity, beyond any caste or religion.

His whole life was given to world peace and universal brotherhood. The Geeta Ashram, Delhi, formed in 1950, now has branches across India and the world, and the annual International Geeta Conference is held at Geeta Dham during Gurudev's birthday celebrations at Holi.
