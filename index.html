About the Mission

Gurudev worked to tap the goodness within people, believing that every community, caste, creed and color shares the same God and the same inner drive for excellence. That spirit is the core of everything Geeta Dham does, with special outreach to young students and professionals, so they can channel their energy into service that brings compassion and joy.
